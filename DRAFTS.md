# Drafts — The 3AM Fridge Text

## Draft A (first idea)
> Hey. It's 3am. The light's on, nobody's home, and I just want you to know the pickles saw everything.
(108 chars)

Rejected: the pickle joke is okay but the setup eats half the line and the punch
("the pickles saw everything") is a bit random — it doesn't land on the fridge's
own voice.

## Draft B (second idea)
> 3am check-in: I've been humming the same note for six years and you still haven't asked what song it is.
(105 chars)

Rejected: good appliance voice (the hum), but it reads more like a sad status
update than a text you'd screenshot and send to a friend. The punch is too soft.

## Final (revised from A + B)
> It's 3am. You opened me 11 times today and took nothing. I am a fridge, not a museum.

Kept the 3am framing and the fridge's small complaining voice, but replaced the
punch with a concrete grievance (counting the pointless openings) and a short,
lock-screen punchline: "I am a fridge, not a museum." Fresh angle — not milk,
ice, or a door left open — and the whole thing reads as one text message.
